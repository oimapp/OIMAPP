# Google Play: what to type in Play Console

Copy each part into the box with the same name in Play Console.

## Store listing (Grow users > Store presence > Main store listing)

**App name**
Ona Iwa Mimo

**Short description** (80 letters at most)
Sunday School Manual, Daily Divine Bread, hymns and Bible in English and Yoruba

**Full description**

The official app of Ona Iwa Mimo Cherubim and Seraphim Church of Nigeria and Overseas.

Read the books of the church on your phone, in English and Yoruba, even without data.

SUNDAY SCHOOL MANUAL
Every lesson for the year, with the memory verse and Bible texts. Teachers get a teacher's area for class registers.

DAILY DIVINE BREAD (OÚNJẸ ÌYÈ OJOOJÚMỌ́)
The reading for each day, with a reminder every morning at the time you choose.

CHILDREN'S SUNDAY SCHOOL MANUAL
The children's lessons for the year.

CHURCH CONSTITUTION
The Constitution of the church in English, and the Òfin in Yoruba.

HYMN BOOK, FREE
866 hymns and 45 Kneeling Songs (Orin Akúnlẹ̀kọ) in English and Yoruba, plus the Ìsọjí songs. Find a hymn by its number, its title, or any words you remember.

THE HOLY BIBLE, FREE
King James Version and the Yoruba Bible, offline. Tap any Bible text in a lesson to open it.

CHURCH LIFE
The church calendar and special days, districts and churches with phone numbers and directions, the National Executive Council, past Baba Aladura and Conference Secretaries, prayer requests, counselling, and testimonies.

ALSO
Notes and bookmarks, read aloud, dark mode, large text.

The books are bought inside the app with Google Play. The hymn book, the Bible and the church pages are free for everyone.

## App content (Policy and programs > App content)

**Privacy policy:** https://oimapp.netlify.app/privacy

**App access:** choose "All or some functionality in the app is restricted". Add instructions:
- Name: Reviewer account
- Username: the email of a test account you create for Google (for example a new Gmail)
- Password: its password
- Notes: "Sign in with this email and password. The account already owns the books." (Unlock all the books for this account in the admin first, with the reason "Google review". Do this again after you press "Take back the books" on launch day.)

**Ads:** No, my app does not contain ads.

**Content rating:** start the questionnaire. Category: "Reference, News, or Educational". Answer No to violence, sexual content, bad language, drugs, gambling. Answer Yes to "users can interact or exchange content" only if asked about sharing: members send prayer requests and testimonies, which the church checks before anything is shown to others.

**Target audience:** choose 13-15, 16-17 and 18 and over. Do not choose any age under 13 (that brings extra rules for children's apps). Answer No to "Could your store listing unintentionally appeal to children".

**News app:** No.

**Data safety:** answer like this.
- Does your app collect or share user data? **Yes**.
- Is all data encrypted in transit? **Yes**.
- Do you provide a way for users to request deletion? **Yes**. Link: https://oimapp.netlify.app/delete-account
- Data collected (none of it is shared with other companies for their own use, none of it is for advertising):
  - Personal info: **Name**, **Email address**, **Phone number** (Account management, App functionality). Required.
  - Financial info: **Purchase history** (App functionality). Required.
  - Messages: **Other in-app messages** (prayer requests, counselling, testimonies) (App functionality). Optional.
  - App activity: **Other user-generated content** (notes and bookmarks) (App functionality). Optional.
  - Device or other IDs: **Device or other IDs** (one account on one phone; notifications) (App functionality, Fraud prevention). Required.

**Government app:** No. **Financial features:** none. **Health:** none.
